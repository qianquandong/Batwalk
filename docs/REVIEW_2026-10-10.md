# Batwalk review — 2026-10-10

An outside review of `main` (TestFlight build 9) and the draft PR
*Unify Watch and iPhone companions with reliable progress and Gentle pixel bats*,
from two angles: a game reviewer's and an Apple platform engineer's. Overall
**5.8 / 10** before changes: engineering A−, art B+, gameplay C+, monetisation D.
Everything below the scorecard was then implemented on `improve/review-fixes`.

Simulation (2,000 runs, 5–8k steps/day, best egg each time): 20 species in
3–5 weeks, 30 in 2.5–4 months, the last two Legendaries 4–6 months more; ≈ 200
hatches to finish, ~170 of them duplicates; median **one** Shiny by completion.

## What changed

**Gameplay**
- Hatch is an action: turn the Digital Crown or tap to crack the shell, light in
  the rarity's colour leaks through, ≈ 2.4 s to the bat, a haptic signature per
  rarity (`HatchViews.swift`, `Haptics.reveal`).
- Pacing (`Tuning`, `GameState`, `EconomyTests`): Shiny 3 % with a guarantee every
  40 hatches and ×3 for Lv.5 species; new-species pity after 10 dry hatches;
  duplicates of a Lv.5 bat pay Moon Dust, 4 dust calls an unmet bat.
- Free tier: one trial Forest Egg once all three free bats are met; Long Ear joins
  the Tiny pool (Q1); the "met every free bat" card and locked eggs open the unlock.
- Optional "Bat Awake" dusk notification (Q8). Oddball Bats gated to content wave 2 (Q12).

**Platform**
- Complication shows the companion portrait + egg ring (cracks from 50 %), keeps
  the last known progress when stale, Smart Stack relevance at hatch-ready and dusk.
- Watch can purchase; entitlement policy: only verified → unlock, only revoked → lock.
- Siri / Shortcuts "Check my egg"; Double Tap on hatch, pet and unlock.
- iPhone follows Light/Dark; locked Batpedia cards open the unlock; share card with
  the real-life-cousin fact; Journal shows Moon Dust.
- One-pixel rim on dark-bodied species.

**Engineering (audit findings)**
1. Step sync falls back to per-day HealthKit queries when buckets don't match the
   ledger's time zone; absence lookback capped at 45 days; daily totals clamped.
2. The iPhone publishes its collection mirror to the Watch over WatchConnectivity,
   so a re-paired Watch restores without iCloud; onboarding offers it.
3. Entitlement checks never re-lock on an unknown result; re-applied after load/restore.
4. Complication freshness survives background wakes; reloads only on visible change.
5. Restore jumps the revision ahead of any mirror; cloud backup rewritten only on
   real content change (no walk-time fingerprint); newer-schema mirrors explained.
6. File-transfer backlog and racing flushes fixed; legacy free-form command path removed.

**Release**
- CI on every PR: core tests on Linux, then Watch + iPhone simulator builds on macOS (free GitHub runners since the repo went public).
- TelemetryDeck sink behind a build setting (`docs/ANALYTICS.md`).
- Price $2.99; String Catalog + strict-concurrency warnings on; privacy/support pages in `site/`.
