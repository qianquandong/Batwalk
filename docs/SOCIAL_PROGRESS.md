# Little meetings: weeks and months

2026-10-23. A collection, never a streak or a ranking.

- Weekly recap uses seven local-calendar days, Monday to Sunday. Monday shows the completed previous week; Sunday shows the current week. Every day has an egg, bat, or Shiny symbol. Multiple hatches count individually, with Shiny taking visual precedence. Empty weeks remain shareable. Legacy Moon Dust arrivals do not count as walked hatches.
- Monthly challenges reward Bronze / Silver / Gold at 1 / 3 / 6 egg hatches (`Tuning.challengeThresholds`). Species pools and Friendship are independent of challenges. Every species remains discoverable in every season.
- October 2026 has Pumpkin Cape; November Acorn Scarf; December Snowflake Collar; January 2027 Starlight Scarf. Later months use Moonlight Scarf. Silver embroiders the month, Gold adds a still glow. No reward expires after it is earned.
- The local month is frozen in each new hatch record. Earned outfits and month counts are stored separately from the journal, so travel or a shortened restore history cannot remove them. Existing history is migrated on the next hatch. Outfit selection belongs to the Watch: Options → Outfit; default Latest, with None and earned month choices.
- Phone reminders are off by default, use local notifications only, and ask permission only after a toggle. Sunday at 09:00 announces the recap. Day 1 introduces the month's outfit; day 15 is scheduled only when the current month's mirrored collection has progress; one reminder on the third-last day. Copy describes the next milestone, without loss or comparison.
- Schedules refresh when the phone opens or its mirror revision changes. iOS does not guarantee background execution, so a reminder may reflect the last mirrored collection. No background polling or server is introduced. Disabling a toggle removes the corresponding schedule.
- Cards are rendered locally. Sharing uses the system share sheet; no automated posting. Week and month cards contain game events only, not Health totals.

Core coverage: day mapping, multiple hatches, Shiny precedence, time zones, DST, empty weeks, month boundaries, frozen months, tier thresholds, no pool changes, outfit save/backup restoration and local reminder scheduling.

Native visual evidence is recorded in `previews/social-progress-2026-10-23/` after the Screenshots workflow completes. Real-device notification delivery and paired-device timing remain TestFlight checks.
