Batwalk Privacy Policy

AI Man Jack LLC · Updated October 10, 2026

Batwalk is a cozy pet game for Apple Watch: your steps hatch eggs, and the bats that hatch keep you company. The game is designed so that your health data stays on your devices.

What Batwalk reads

Step Count from Apple Health, with your permission, to grow your egg and find fruit. Batwalk reads only your daily step total. It never writes to Health and never reads location, workouts, heart rate or any other Health record.

Where your data lives

  • Your game and step accounting are stored on your Apple Watch. Health-derived files are excluded from device backup.
  • Your paired iPhone receives an offline mirror of the game through Apple's WatchConnectivity, and keeps a copy of your collection (bats, names, friendship, hatch history) so a new Watch can restore it. The mirror never includes step history.
  • If the build has iCloud backup enabled, only that collection list is stored in your private iCloud account. Daily steps, step dates, lifetime steps and egg progress are never uploaded.

What Batwalk does not do

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no data brokers, no Batwalk servers.
  • No Health information is ever sent anywhere.
  • Purchases are handled and verified by Apple. We receive no payment details.

Anonymous game statistics

Some builds count anonymous game events (for example "egg hatched" or "bat fed") through TelemetryDeck so we can tell whether the game is fun. These counts contain no step data, no identifiers and no Health information, and they are never sold. If a build has this switched on, the Privacy & data screen inside the app says so.

Siri and the watch face

The watch-face complication and Siri's "Check my egg" read the same small egg-progress summary (companion name, steps to hatch) stored on your device.

Your choices

  • Change Step Count access any time in the Health app on iPhone.
  • Change notifications in your device settings.
  • Deleting Batwalk deletes its local data. If iCloud backup is enabled, manage that copy in your iCloud storage settings.

Children

Batwalk collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
