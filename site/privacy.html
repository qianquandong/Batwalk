Batwalk Privacy Policy

AI Man Jack LLC · Updated October 23, 2026

Batwalk is a cozy pet game for Apple Watch: your steps hatch eggs, and the bats that hatch keep you company. The game is designed so that your health data stays on your devices.

What Batwalk reads

Step Count from Apple Health, with your permission, to grow your egg and find fruit. Batwalk reads only your daily step total. It never writes to Health and never reads location, workouts, heart rate or any other Health record.

Where your data lives

  • Your game and step accounting are stored on your Apple Watch. Health-derived files are excluded from device backup.
  • Your paired iPhone receives an offline mirror of the game through Apple's WatchConnectivity, and keeps a copy of your collection (bats, names, friendship, hatch history) so a new Watch can restore it. The mirror never includes step history, step totals, active egg progress, or certificate steps and start dates.
  • If the build has iCloud backup enabled, only that collection list is stored in your private iCloud account. Daily steps, step dates, lifetime steps and egg progress are never uploaded.

Cards you choose to share

Birth certificates and collection posters are rendered on your iPhone. By default, a certificate has no walk details. Tapping Include my steps and days asks your paired Watch for that one receipt; it stays in memory during the preview, never in mirrors, collection backups, analytics, or links. The phone-only Show steps on shared cards setting defaults on, but does not itself request or send any Health information. Turn it off to remove both steps and days. You choose whether and where to send the card using Apple's share sheet. Old walks may have no saved details. Collection posters have no Health information.

What Batwalk does not do

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no data brokers, no Batwalk servers.
  • No Health information is sent automatically. Sharing a card with walk details is always your choice.
  • This free version makes no purchases. Historical purchases were handled by Apple; we receive no payment details.

Anonymous game statistics

Some builds count anonymous game events (for example "egg hatched" or "bat fed") through TelemetryDeck so we can tell whether the game is fun. These counts contain no step data, no identifiers and no Health information, and they are never sold. If a build has this switched on, the Privacy & data screen inside the app says so.

Siri and the watch face

In builds with live shared progress enabled, the watch-face complication and Siri's "Check my egg" read a small egg-progress summary stored on your device. The initial release does not enable this shared progress; its complication opens Batwalk instead. No summary is sent to developer servers.

Birthday hats

Birthday hats are optional. Only month and day are stored, never your birth year. Your iPhone sends that preference to the paired Watch; it is included in your paired collection and private iCloud backup when enabled. Hat years record when you earned a keepsake, not your age. Remove the birthday in iPhone Settings whenever you like; earned hats remain yours. No birthday information is sent to developer servers.

Your choices

  • Change Step Count access any time in the Health app on iPhone.
  • Change notifications in your device settings.
  • Deleting Batwalk deletes its local data. If iCloud backup is enabled, manage that copy in your iCloud storage settings.

Children

Batwalk collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
